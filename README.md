## 〔 WHO AM I 〕

</div>

<br/>

I'm a **full-stack developer** who genuinely enjoys every layer of the stack. From building smooth, responsive UIs with **React.js** to designing REST APIs, architecting databases, and reasoning about how systems scale — I care about all of it equally.

I don't specialize in just one area by choice. I believe the best developers understand the entire product — how the frontend talks to the backend, how the backend talks to the database, and how all of it holds together under real load. **Versatility backed by strong fundamentals** is what I'm building toward.

I'm constantly working on projects, solving DSA problems, reading codebases, and finding new problems worth solving. Always building. Always learning. Always shipping.
